Who funds what, and what they need from you first.

Every funder in the directory, with all of its programs in one place — plus the registrations and portals that have to be sorted out weeks before you can submit anything.

Before you can submit anything

Registration failures are the most avoidable way to miss a deadline, and they take weeks, not days. Check these the day you decide to apply.

SystemWhat it isWho sets it upLead time
SAM.govFederal entity registration; issues the UEI. Required for every federal grant and contract.Institution (sponsored programs). Renews annually.Weeks. An expired registration blocks submission entirely and renewal is not instant.
Grants.govSubmission portal for most federal discretionary grants.Institution assigns roles; the AOR must be authorized to submit.Days to weeks for role assignment.
eRA CommonsNIH (and several other PHS agencies). Every PI needs a personal account linked to the institution.Institutional signing official creates the account.Days. Do it before you have a project.
Research.govNSF proposal preparation, submission, and reviewer work. Also where Data Management and Sharing Plans now live.Institution adds you; you need an NSF ID.Days.
NSPIRESNASA proposals.Self-register, then link to the institution.Days.
ProposalCentralUsed by many private biomedical funders and some federal programs. If your funder uses it, you have no alternative.Self-register.Same day, but budget time to learn it.
Foundation portalsMost foundations run their own system (Fluxx, SM Apply, Submittable, or a bespoke one).Self-register, but some require an invitation or a prior LOI.Varies — check before the LOI deadline, not after.
SAM.gov contract opportunitiesFederal contracts, including evaluation and technical assistance work. Not on Grants.gov.Same SAM registration.Already done if SAM is current.
The private foundation trap. Many foundations require a 501(c)(3) determination letter. A public university is a state instrumentality, not a 501(c)(3), so it often cannot apply under its own EIN and must go through an affiliated foundation. Sort out which entity applies before you write the cover page. At VSU there are three separate exempt entities — see the VSU Roadmap.
