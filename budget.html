Budget calculator

A budget that survives review.

Most budget errors are not arithmetic — they are applying indirect costs to the wrong base, forgetting an exclusion, or promising effort the calendar cannot hold. This does the mechanics with VSU’s published rates and flags the traps at the point where you would otherwise walk into them.

The on-campus F&A rate is 44% of modified total direct costs, confirmed current as of 2026-08-06. Off-campus is 44% MTDC, cognizant agency DHHS. One practical note that has nothing to do with whether the rate is right: some sponsors ask for the signed rate agreement PDF as an attachment, so it is worth having a copy from the Office of Sponsored Research & Programs in your proposal file before you need it.

Second trap, from VSU’s own wording: 37% is the fringe benefit rate, not the indirect rate. VSU’s salary page says the indirect cost rate agreement “has established a fringe benefit rate of 37%,” which reads to a lot of people as though 37% were the F&A rate. It is not.

Setup

Personnel

Effort is entered the way the sponsor asks for it. Person-months is the NIH convention; percent effort is common at NSF and most foundations. Nine-month faculty have two separate pools — academic-year effort and summer — and they are capped differently.

Other direct costs

Watch the MTDC column. Modified total direct cost is the base F&A is charged on, and four things come out of it: capital equipment, tuition, participant support, and the portion of each subaward above $25,000. Getting these wrong is the single most common budget error.
